👋 About me [Updated 25/03/2026]

I am Hang Yu (余航). I completed my bachelor's degree at Zhejiang Sci-Tech University under Prof. Yang Bin, my master's degree at Sichuan Normal University under Prof. Wang Yifeng, and I am currently a PhD student in Cognitive Neuroscience at East China Normal University under Prof. Yang Qianli. Before that, I worked as a research assistant at CIBR and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, both highly formative experiences.

I entered academia to pursue what genuinely interests me. My current research focuses on computational modeling of human cognition; I aim to use probabilistic graphical models and reinforcement learning to uncover how humans solve complex problems, and I will gradually share what I learn from psychology.

I welcome learning exchanges and research collaborations. If our interests align, feel free to 📧 email me.
