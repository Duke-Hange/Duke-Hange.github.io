👋 About Me [Updated March 25, 2026]

I am Hang Yu. I completed my undergraduate degree at Zhejiang Sci-Tech University under the supervision of Bin Yang, and my master’s degree at Sichuan Normal University under Yifeng Wang. I am now a PhD student in cognitive neuroscience at East China Normal University, supervised by Qianli Yang. I have also gained valuable research experience as a research assistant at the Chinese Institute for Brain Research, Beijing, and Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I embarked on an academic career to pursue the questions that most interest me. My current focus is on computational modeling of human cognition. I aim to use probabilistic graphical models and reinforcement learning to understand how humans solve complex problems. I also hope to share what I learn from psychology along the way.

I welcome academic exchange and research collaboration. If our interests align, please 📧 get in touch by email.
